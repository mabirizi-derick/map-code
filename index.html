💡 About the Creator & Project Vision

The Kyambogo Digital Campus Map was envisioned, designed, and developed by Derick Mabirizi to solve one of the most persistent everyday challenges for students, lecturers, new freshmen, and visitors: finding lecture halls, administrative wings, departments, and specific staff offices across the sprawling hills of Kyambogo University.

Derick created this system with modern geospatial navigation algorithms, real-time GPS location tracking, verified campus building boundaries, and offline mobile capability — ensuring students never miss a lecture or struggle to locate an office again.
